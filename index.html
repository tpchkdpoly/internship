Internship Facilitation & Forwarding Workflow

Your Internship Journey – From Planning to Approval

Follow these nine stages carefully to complete the internship facilitation and forwarding process at Hailakandi Polytechnic.

Preparatory stage Mandatory stage
Phase A Planning & Exploration
01
Stage 01Preparatory

Group Formation / Individual Decision

Students decide whether they wish to undertake an internship individually or as a group.

Group members should coordinate among themselves and finalise their participation before approaching prospective organisations.

02
Stage 02Preparatory

Identify Potential Internship Opportunities

Explore possible internship opportunities through different avenues relevant to your branch.

Possible avenues include:

  • Industries and manufacturing organisations
  • Government departments and establishments
  • MSMEs and private companies
  • Research institutions and laboratories
  • Workshops and technical organisations
  • Other relevant organisations

Students should identify suitable opportunities according to their branch, technical interests and internship requirements.

03
Stage 03Preparatory

Establish Initial / Informal Communication

Identify the strongest lead or primary contact within the group and establish informal communication with the prospective organisation.

Understand the organisation's willingness to accommodate students for internship training. Students should clarify:

  • Availability of internship opportunities
  • Number of students that can be accommodated
  • Relevant department or technical area
  • Tentative internship duration
  • Expected commencement date
This stage is intended for initial communication and opportunity exploration, not a substitute for official institutional forwarding.
Phase B Official Forwarding & Approval
04
Stage 04 Mandatory

Collect Organisation and Forwarding Details

Before submitting the application, collect and verify all mandatory organisation and forwarding details — including the official email address.

  • Name of the internship organisation
  • Complete internship venue address
  • Name of the authority/person to whom the forwarding letter must be addressed
  • Designation of the recipient
  • Complete official forwarding letter address
  • Official email address of the recipient/organisation Mandatory
Important: Students must ensure that the forwarding address and official email address are accurate. Incomplete or incorrect information may delay the processing of the forwarding letter.
05
Stage 05 Mandatory

Submit Application to the TPO Office

Complete the official Internship Forwarding Application Google Form and submit all required information.

The application will be received by the Training & Placement Cell for verification and processing.

Submit Internship Application
06
Stage 06 Mandatory

Verify Release of Forwarding Letter

Follow up with the Training & Placement Officer regarding processing and release of the official forwarding letter.

After submission, students should follow up with the Training & Placement Officer regarding the processing and release of the official internship forwarding letter. Students must collect the forwarding letter after it has been officially issued.

07
Stage 07 Mandatory

Submit Internship Offer Letter / Confirmation

Obtain an official offer letter or written confirmation from the organisation and submit it to the TPO Office.

After receiving the forwarding letter, students should obtain an official internship offer letter or written confirmation from the organisation. Submit the offer letter or confirmation to the Training & Placement Cell for institutional records.

08
Stage 08 Mandatory

Obtain Departmental NOC

Approach your Head of Department (HOD) and obtain the required No Objection Certificate (NOC).

After submitting the internship offer letter to the TPO Office, students must approach their respective Head of Department (HOD) and obtain the required No Objection Certificate (NOC).

09
Stage 09 Mandatory

Final Documentation and Preparation

Ensure all necessary documents have been collected and retained before commencing your internship.

Required documents:

Official forwarding letter issued by the institute
Internship offer letter or confirmation from the organisation
Departmental No Objection Certificate (NOC)

Complete all required documentation and coordinate with the Training & Placement Cell and your respective department before commencing your internship.

Stage 05 · Online Submission

Submit Your Application

Internship Forwarding Application

Students intending to undertake mandatory or voluntary internships must submit their internship forwarding details through the official online application form.

  1. Keep the organisation details, recipient designation and official email address ready.
  2. Fill in every field of the Google Form accurately.
  3. Submit the form and note your application reference for future correspondence.
Submit Internship Application

Opens the official Google Form in a new tab.

Scan the QR code to access the official internship application form.

Track Your Internship Application

Students can check the progress of their submitted internship forwarding application through the official application status tracker maintained by the Training & Placement Cell.

  1. Open the status tracker using the button below.
  2. Locate your entry using your Application ID.
  3. Check the current status and follow up with the TPO Office if required.
Check Application Status

Opens the read-only tracker in a new tab.

What the tracker shows

  • Application ID
  • Student name
  • Branch
  • Submission date
  • Application status

The tracker is a read-only view. Phone numbers, email addresses, sensitive student details and internal remarks are never displayed.

Read Before You Apply

Important Instructions for Students

Explore and identify suitable internship opportunities well in advance.

Establish initial communication with the prospective organisation.

Collect the complete forwarding address and mandatory official email address.

Ensure that all details submitted in the Google Form are accurate.

Submit the application before the prescribed deadline.

Keep the application reference number for future correspondence.

Regularly check the application status.

Collect the official forwarding letter after it is issued.

Submit the internship offer letter or confirmation to the TPO Office.

Obtain the required NOC from the respective Head of Department.

Maintain copies of all internship-related documents.

Have a question? Contact the Training & Placement Cell.

Disclaimer

Submission of the internship application or receipt of an institutional forwarding letter does not itself constitute final institutional approval. Students must complete the prescribed documentation and obtain the necessary departmental clearance before commencing their internship.
